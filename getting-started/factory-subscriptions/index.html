Use Your Subscription In Factory

Your own conversations in Mastra Factory run on your own Codex (ChatGPT) or Claude subscription. Board work, the agents planning and building cards, runs on X4's shared model access, so your cards get worked whether or not you've connected a subscription. X4 sign-in and provider sign-in are separate; you don't need an API key.

Rollout: this applies once Factory 0.30 is deployed to Factory.

What You Need​

SubscriptionPlans that work
Codex / ChatGPTA paid ChatGPT plan (Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise). A free ChatGPT account is refused by OpenAI.
ClaudeA Claude Pro or Max plan.

One connected subscription is enough. Connect both if you want to choose between OpenAI and Anthropic models.

Connect Your Account​

  1. Connect Tailscale and open Mastra Factory. Your Tailscale identity signs you in to Factory.
  2. Open your project's Settings → Models (https://factory.x4app.dev/factories/<project>/settings/models). Model routing shows Hybrid: chats use your personal defaults, and work-item runs use the Factory's.
  3. Under Personal defaults → Providers, click Add provider. In Sign in with a subscription, click Sign in next to OpenAI or Anthropic (not API key). If your provider is already listed, you're done.
  4. Complete your provider's flow, then return to Factory:
SubscriptionSign-in flow
Codex / ChatGPTOpen the authorization link, sign in to your ChatGPT account, and enter the device code. Wait for Factory to confirm the connection.
ClaudeOpen the authorization link, sign in to your Claude account, and paste the authorization code into Factory when prompted.

Factory refreshes the sign-in on its own. If your subscription lapses or you revoke Factory's access, connect again.

Whose Account Runs What​

WorkRuns on
A conversation you openYour subscription.
Board work: Planning, Building and reviews on a card, whoever started itX4's shared model access, with the project's model. It runs on the devbox of the person who last moved the card, and opens in their X4 Code.

This is Factory's Hybrid model routing, which X4 uses for every project (see the deployment guide). Your plan only matters for your own conversations: a card on a GPT project is worked even if you have only Claude, or a free ChatGPT account.

Check That It Works​

  1. While signed in, open provider status. Find your provider and check source: "oauth-user" and userCredential: "oauth".

  2. Start a new conversation and send:

    Run pwd and git branch --show-current, then reply SUBSCRIPTION_TEST_OK. Do not change any files.

  3. Confirm the shell tool succeeds and the reply contains the marker.

A successful tool run confirms inference and tools work on your subscription. Provider status shows what is stored; it doesn't prove provider-side billing.

If It Does Not Work​

  • Not logged in… or an OpenAI or Anthropic sign-in error: you have no working connection for that provider (Factory also says None of your connected accounts can run …). Connect it under Settings → Models → Add provider, then retry.
  • Your plan refuses the conversation's model: your conversation shows the provider's error. Factory doesn't switch your conversation to shared access; pick a model your plan runs, or connect your other provider. (Board work isn't affected: it always runs on shared access.)
  • … not supported when using Codex with a ChatGPT account: your ChatGPT plan is free. Use a paid plan, or connect Claude instead.
  • stored-user: your personal connection is an API key, not a subscription. Sign in with your subscription instead.
  • Quota or rate limits: these come from your provider plan. Wait, or connect your other provider and choose one of its models.
X4 test status and hosted-provider support

On November 9, 2026, an existing personal OpenAI connection passed a live conversation and shell-tool call. Our Anthropic connection returned Not logged in to Anthropic; successful inference remains unverified. Fresh sign-in, renewal and billing attribution still need validation on Factory 0.30.

Factory provides both connectors natively. Provider support for hosted usage is a separate question: the pinned OpenAI connector differs from the documented hosted Sign in with ChatGPT integration. Anthropic restricts third-party subscription-token inference and documents hosting unmodified Claude Code separately.
